Our Journey

From pager to platform

Company timeline, major milestones, and product growth.

2019

Born from a 3am incident

Three SREs watch a slow database take down production while paging through 6 disconnected tools. The first Stackly prototype ships a week later.

2020

First 100 teams

Word spreads among early users. Stackly unifies metrics, logs, and traces for its first 100 SaaS teams — with zero infrastructure of their own.

2022

AI arrives

We train anomaly detection on billions of signals, cutting alert noise by 87% for early customers. MTTR drops below 15 minutes.

2024

Enterprise scale

SOC 2, ISO 27001, and data residency in 9 regions. Fortune 500 teams adopt Stackly for mission-critical workloads.

2026

10,000+ servers, 5,000+ customers

Today Stackly processes 48 billion events a day and keeps some of the world's busiest SaaS platforms online.
